WORDS OF REMEMBRANCE

Duas & supplications

A small starter collection for your journey.

QUR'AN · AL-BAQARAH 2:201
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Transliteration
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

Meaning
Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

TALBIYAH
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ

Transliteration
Labbayka Allahumma labbayk.

Meaning
Here I am, O Allah, here I am.

This is an abbreviated opening of the Talbiyah. Consult a reliable dua source for the complete wording and guidance on when it is recited.
